30Wed, December

A Chianti lunch, the perfect day trip

Il CartografoOut through the vineyards to an abbey. I have the map; you bring the appetite.

Pranzo

Osteria di Passignano

Antinori’s Michelin-starred country restaurant sits beside the 11th-century Badia di Passignano, in the heart of Chianti Classico. Expect a refined Tuscan lunch among the vines, with wine from the cellars under the abbey.

  • Drive: about 40 minutes south of Florence. Leave by 11:30 AM.
  • Lunch: served 12:15 to 2:15 PM on Wednesdays. Book ahead and confirm holiday hours.
  • Tip: choose a designated driver, since the cellar is the point.
